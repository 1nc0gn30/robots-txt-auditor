Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 4, 2026 • 100% Client-Side Privacy Commitment

1. The Zero-Telemetry Guarantee

At robots-txt-auditor and Neal Frazier Tech, your code, data, and keys remain strictly yours. We believe modern developer utilities should operate without surveillance.

Absolute Local Isolation: 100% of computational logic, AST scanning, code transformations, and file generations occur directly within your local browser execution context. No data ever leaves your device.

2. What We Collect (And What We Don't)

  • We DO NOT collect: Your source code, configuration files, secrets, prompts, or AST outputs.
  • We DO NOT run: Third-party tracking scripts, advertising trackers, or behavioral session recording software.
  • Local Storage Only: We store your Pro activation status and workspace drafts in your browser's local localStorage purely for state persistence.

3. Payment & Checkout Information

When purchasing a Pro commercial license:

  • Stripe processes your credit card / digital wallet data under industry-standard PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance. We never see or store your full card details.
  • DePay / Solana processes transactions directly on the public blockchain without intermediate custodial accounts.

4. Compliance & Contact

Our architecture inherently complies with GDPR, CCPA, and CPRA standards because we do not ingest, transfer, or commercialize your personal information. For any privacy inquiries, reach us directly at neal@nealfrazier.tech.
